🌍 Air & Water Pollution Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes air quality and water pollution data at both country and city levels. The dashboard helps identify best and worst performing regions, track pollution trends, and support data-driven environmental insights.

📊 Key Features

Country-level & city-level pollution analysis

KPI cards for:

Average Air Pollution

Average Water Pollution

Total Countries / Cities

Best & worst air quality comparison by country

Best & worst water quality comparison by country and city

Interactive map visualization for geographic insights

Dynamic filters (Region, Country, City)

Drill-through enabled for deeper analysis

🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

Power BI – Data modeling, DAX measures, dashboards

Excel / CSV – Data source & preprocessing

DAX – KPI calculations and aggregations

Data Visualization – Bar charts, cards, maps, slicers

🎯 Objective

To transform raw environmental data into meaningful insights that help:

Identify pollution hotspots

Compare environmental quality across regions

Support policy analysis and sustainability discussions

📌 Insights Provided

Countries with the best and worst air quality

Cities with highest and lowest water quality

Regional pollution distribution using maps

Clear KPI-based summary for quick decision-making
